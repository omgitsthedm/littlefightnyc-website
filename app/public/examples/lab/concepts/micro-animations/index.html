Micro-interactions are the difference between software people tolerate and software people love. Everything in the little app below answers your finger with real physics — instantly, from wherever it currently is, at the speed you threw it.

This is the finish we put on working software: buttons that press back, sheets that catch your throw, deletes that make you mean it, mistakes that cost nothing. Each one is invisible on its own. Together they’re why a product feels expensive.

Seven moments

  1. PressButtons acknowledge on touch-down, not on release.
  2. ThrowThe sheet catches your flick and lands where you meant.
  3. HoldDestructive actions ask you to mean it.
  4. UndoMistakes cost nothing — the toast rides your swipe.
  5. SlideTabs move their color, not just their underline.
  6. FlipLight to dark without a hard cut.
  7. CommitErrors shake. Success settles.

All seven felt. Free play — break nothing, feel everything.

After Hours Agenda has run on pink since 2011. Here the lockup becomes a physical object: a laser traces every letterform live, the tubes catch like real neon, and the whole sign hangs on a pull-chain you can actually pull.

The letterforms and the pink are AHA's own — brand is law. The same light system dresses storefront signage, menu boards, merch drops, and event flyers without redrawing a thing.

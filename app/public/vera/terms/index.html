Terms of use

An instrument, not an agent.

VERA reads public listings and public records, scores what it finds, and shows its work. It does not represent you, does not represent a landlord, and does not take a cut of anything.

Effective 29 July 2026 · VERA is operated by Little Fight NYC

What VERA is

A research and analysis tool for people looking for an apartment in New York City. It aggregates listings that are already publicly posted, cross-references them against public records, and presents an opinionated evaluation with the reasoning attached.

What VERA is not

  • Not a real-estate broker or agent. It is not licensed, it does not show apartments, negotiate, or represent either side of a transaction, and it earns nothing from any lease you sign.
  • Not a listing service. It does not host inventory. Every listing links back to its original source, which is the authority on price, availability, and terms.
  • Not legal advice. Where these pages describe New York rent law, that is general information, current to the best of our knowledge on the effective date above, and it is not a substitute for a tenant attorney or a housing counsellor.
  • Not financial advice. The calculators are arithmetic on figures you supply and on common landlord conventions. They are not an assessment of what you can afford.

Accuracy, and the limits of it

Listing data is only ever as good as its source, and rental listings are frequently stale, duplicated, mispriced, or fabricated. VERA is explicitly built around that fact — it scores authenticity, flags what it could not verify, and clusters duplicates — but it cannot guarantee that any listing is real, available, accurately priced, or legally rentable.

Public-record enrichment depends on matching a listing’s address to a building record. That match can fail or land on the wrong building. Where confidence is low, VERA says so. Treat every score as a starting point for your own verification, never as a conclusion. Verify the unit, the landlord, and the lease in person and in writing before you pay anyone anything.

Nothing here should be the last thing you check. A high score means VERA found no problems in the data it can see. It cannot see the water pressure, the neighbours, or the person you are about to hand a deposit to.

Acceptable use

  • Use VERA to find a home for yourself. Personal, non-commercial use.
  • Do not scrape, mirror, or resell the published data, or use it to build a competing commercial index.
  • Do not use it to harass, profile, or target any landlord, owner, or occupant. Public records describe buildings, not people to pursue.
  • Do not present VERA’s evaluations as your own professional opinion to a third party.

Grades, corrections, and fair housing

Steward grades and every verification signal are computed from cited public records; each failure names its source where it appears. Owners and agents can have misreadings corrected — and disputed records answered verbatim — via the corrections page. VERA never uses race, religion, national origin, familial status, disability, source of income, or any protected characteristic in scoring or curation; neighborhood focus reflects one operator’s personal search map, not a judgment about who should live anywhere.

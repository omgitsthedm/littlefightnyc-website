Corrections

VERA's steward grades are computed, not opined. Every grade is derived from cited public records — HPD housing-maintenance violations and complaints, DOB records, 311 heat and hot-water complaint patterns, bedbug filings under Local Law 69, and HPD buildings litigation — and every failure a grade names links to its source on the page where it appears.

If you own or manage a graded building

The fastest correction is at the source: if a violation is cleared, a complaint resolved, or a registration current, update the record with HPD or DOB — VERA re-reads the city's data on every sweep and the grade follows the record automatically, usually within a day of the city's own refresh.

If you believe VERA is misreading a record — wrong building, wrong join, stale data — write to info@afterhoursagenda.com with the address and the specific claim. A human reads every message. If the reading is wrong, it gets fixed. If the record is disputed, your response is appended verbatim beside the grade — VERA does not editorialize over an owner's stated position.

What VERA will not do

VERA never contacts owners, never solicits, and never removes accurate public records from view. Grades are never edited by hand in either direction — they are recomputed from the record, or the reading is corrected, or a response is attached. There is no payment, relationship, or request that changes a grade.

Fair housing

VERA scores buildings and listings from public records and listing data only. It never uses race, religion, national origin, familial status, disability, source of income, or any protected characteristic in scoring or curation. Neighborhood focus reflects one operator's personal search map — not a judgment about who should live anywhere.

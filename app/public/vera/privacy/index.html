Privacy

Your hunt never leaves your browser.

Most rental sites are lead-generation businesses wearing a search box — the product is you, sold to a broker. VERA has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and nowhere to send anything even if it wanted to.

Effective 29 July 2026 · VERA is operated by Little Fight NYC

The short version. We do not ask who you are. We do not set a cookie. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts. Saved listings, notes, and viewing checklists are written to your own browser's local storage and are never transmitted anywhere. Clear your browser data and they are gone — including from us, because we never had them.

What we collect

Nothing personal. There is no sign-up, no email field, no profile, no password. The console is a static page that reads a public data file.

Our web host (Netlify) produces standard server logs — IP address, timestamp, requested file, user agent — as an automatic function of serving any website. We do not link those to a person, do not use them to build a profile, and do not sell or share them. They exist for security and uptime.

What stays on your device

When you save a listing to My hunt, VERA writes to localStorage under two keys:

  • vera-cases — the listings you saved, the stage you put each one in, your notes, and your viewing-checklist ticks.
  • vera-workspace — your filter preferences, so the console opens where you left it.

That data never crosses the network. It is not synced, not backed up, and not readable by us. It is also therefore fragile: it is tied to one browser on one device, and clearing site data erases it permanently.

What we publish

The console reads a single sanitised file, public.json. Before anything is published, a step in our pipeline removes the operator's personal layer — contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, private working notes, and any internal watch-list state — and neutralises subjective language about named owners. Building records, violation counts, litigation counts, and rent-stabilisation status remain, because those are public records.

Where the data comes from

VERA indexes rental listings that are already publicly posted, and enriches them with New York City and State public records — including Housing Preservation and Development violation history, Department of Buildings records, housing-court signals, and rent-stabilisation registries. It links to the original listing rather than reproducing it.

Third parties

There are none embedded in the page. No advertising network, no tag manager, no session recorder, no social widget, no font CDN — the typefaces are served from our own origin. When you click through to an original listing, you leave VERA and enter that site's privacy regime, which is very likely nothing like this one.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no account to export, correct, or delete. You are in full control of the only data that exists: clear it in your browser at any time. If you have a question about this policy, contact Little Fight NYC through the main site.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above changes with it. Given the architecture — no accounts, no collection — any future change that started collecting personal data would require rebuilding the product, and we would say so plainly rather than editing a paragraph.

Where the designs fit.

  • Facebook and Instagram: portrait feed concepts. Stories and Reels need their own composition and placement previews.
  • Google Search: the separate headlines and descriptions are ready for copy review. Search image assets require different artwork without added text or logos.
  • Google Performance Max: images with overlays are allowed. These directions can be adapted into the required image shapes, alongside clean versions without overlays.
  • Google Display: prepare the sizes and exports for the chosen Display format before uploading.

These are creative-review files, not a claim of approval by Meta or Google. Final settings, destination checks, any required AI disclosures and platform review still apply.

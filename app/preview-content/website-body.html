Made to fit you.

Little Fight shapes the design, writes the words, builds the pages, and connects the technology around the way your business actually works.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Both platforms include useful tools. A custom build gives Little Fight room to tailor the pages, tools, and content around how your business works. Wix sites run on Wix, while Squarespace exports some content rather than every platform feature and style. Read Wix’s export guidance and Squarespace’s export guidance.

What do you need from me?

You bring the business facts, photos, access, and approvals. Little Fight handles the design, writing, build, setup, and launch work.

A question needs
an answer.

People may arrive through search, a shared link, or a recommendation; the page needs to make the next step clear.

  1. A real question

    “Do they do this?” “Will this work for me?” “What happens next?”

  2. A useful page

    Clear details, real work, and what a person needs to decide.

  3. A reason to trust it

    Specific language and accurate business details.

  4. A human next step

    A clear way to call, text, email, book, or ask.

Little Fight writes the answer pages, connects them, checks the technical basics, and measures meaningful contact actions.

What supports search and AI discovery?

Useful pages need descriptive titles, direct headings, accurate business details, internal links, and a site search engines can crawl. We connect Search Console, use a sitemap, and add structured business information when it fits. Google says its normal SEO guidance also applies to AI search features; there is no special switch that guarantees a recommendation. Read Google’s guidance.

What do we measure?

Calls, texts, emails, forms, and booking clicks can show how visitors move through the site. A click is interest, not a completed job. You tell us what became a real inquiry or customer so we can improve the pages around what is working for you.

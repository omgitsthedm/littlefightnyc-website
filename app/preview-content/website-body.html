What supports search and AI discovery?

Useful pages need descriptive titles, direct headings, accurate business details, internal links, and a site search engines can crawl. We connect Search Console, use a sitemap, and add structured business information when it fits. Google says its normal SEO guidance also applies to AI search features; there is no special switch that guarantees a recommendation. Read Google’s guidance.

What do we measure?

Calls, texts, emails, forms, and booking clicks can show how visitors move through the site. A click is interest, not a completed job. You tell us what became a real inquiry or customer so we can improve the pages around what is working for you.

# Public evidence and claim ledger

This ledger is the approval gate for public numbers and comparative claims.
It is intentionally small: a claim appears here before it appears in customer
copy. Its public presentation must still place a clickable source beside the
claim.

| Claim / use | Evidence type | Source or method | Public rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner Math: missed-inquiry estimate | Visitor inputs | `missed inquiries per week × average sale × close rate × 4 weeks` | Show the formula. Label it an estimate. No external benchmark is used. |
| Client Lighthouse result | Dated client measurement | Lighthouse version, device/form factor, URL, run date, retained in `.lifi/evidence/lighthouse/` | Link to the public case and method. Never present it as a conversion result. |
| Website timing promise | Approved Little Fight commercial term | Written eligibility, timing, dependency, and remedy terms | Do not shorten it to an unconditional guarantee. |
| Urgent New York on-site response (24 hours) | Approved Little Fight commercial term | Written eligibility (urgent, New York, needs hands on site), what starts the clock, what the owner provides, and the remedy if missed | Publish as a typical response, never an unconditional guarantee. The hedge and the conditions appear beside the figure — the homepage quadrant carries "usually within" and the 9am–9pm Eastern window on the same screen. |
| U.S. hourly compensation context, if later needed | External benchmark | [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employer Costs for Employee Compensation](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm) | Optional U.S. context only; the owner enters their own loaded cost. |
| Google Business Profile / Maps product behavior, if later needed | Platform documentation | [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/) | Cite the exact help page for the claimed feature; do not infer rank or revenue. |

## Non-negotiables

- A source link belongs next to the published claim, not only here.
- No statistic becomes a Little Fight promise.
- Client data, private systems, real submissions, payments, and private
  analytics are never public proof.
- If a source goes stale, remove or refresh the claim before the next release.

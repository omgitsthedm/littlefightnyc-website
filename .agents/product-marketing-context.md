# Product Marketing Context — Little Fight NYC

## What we sell
Custom websites, on-site tech support (New York), tech consulting, and small
custom software builds — for independent businesses.

## Who it's for
Independent business owners: cafés, restaurants, salons, barbers, plumbers,
roofers, electricians, law firms, shops, makers, animal hospitals, small
offices. Not startups, not enterprises, not chains.

Geography: websites and software nationwide; tech support on-site in New York
City; strong ties to Martha's Vineyard, Arizona, Louisiana, South Texas, and
Florida's Space Coast.

## What they're feeling before they find us
- Burned before: a freelancer disappeared, or an agency holds their domain hostage
- Invisible: missing from Google Maps while competitors show up
- Embarrassed: their site is slow, broken on phones, or years out of date
- Overwhelmed: too many subscriptions, tools, and passwords, no one to ask
- Suspicious of agencies: they expect jargon, upsells, and surprise invoices

## The transformation
From "my website and tech are a liability I avoid thinking about" to
"I own my domain, my site brings me customers, and I have someone to call."

## Proof points
- Five-star Google reviews: "easy to work with," "quick to respond,"
  "clear communication, expectations and timelines," "a painless experience"
- Real client roster: Chromatic Painting & Design, Hair By Rachel,
  Easy Tiger Bar, All Pets Animal Hospital, The Tarot Hotline, Logan Loans,
  CC Films, Grand Funding LLC, Pole Position IT
- Own products built and shipped: After Hours Agenda, VenueCircuit, VERA

## Differentiators (things competitors can't honestly say)
1. You own everything — domain, hosting, logins — documented and handed over
2. On-site tech support in New York, not just remote tickets
3. We tell you when NOT to rebuild, NOT to buy, NOT to build custom
4. Copy included — we write the words with you, from your real answers
5. The smallest useful change is priced before the big one

## Voice
Calm, plain, specific. A competent friend who happens to build websites.
Never hype, never fear. Questions named in the customer's own words.
Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. No exclamation points.

## Objections and answers
- "Agencies are expensive" → scope stated after one conversation, no surprise line items
- "I'll lose control" → every account in your name, checklist handed over at launch
- "My nephew can do it" → who owns the domain? who answers when the form breaks?
- "AI can build my site now" → AI can't verify your Google listing, shoot your
  photos, or answer the phone when it breaks

## CTA hierarchy
1. Call / Text / Email — persistent, low-pressure
2. "Ask us a question" — matches the question-led brand

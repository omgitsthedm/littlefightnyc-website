## Findings and repair iterations

1. The first desktop hero wrapped the intended three-line promise into five lines and pushed its decisions below a short viewport. The final scale preserves the three-line hierarchy and keeps both decisions visible.
2. The generated mobile reference looked dramatic but spent too much vertical space on its headline. The final mobile hero keeps the shop scene and confidence while showing Call, website help, Text, Email, Form, and human hours in the first screen at 320×568, 375×667, and 390×844.
3. The first work implementation behaved like three equal cards. The final board makes CC Films dominant, stacks Hair By Rachel Charles and ClearHelp as supporting proof, keeps every fact canonical, and keeps homepage project clicks inside the Little Fight case stories. Each live case page retains its verified source link.
4. The connected-system section now shows the five broken handoffs and one accountable Plan → Build → Connect → Keep Working path. Replay is optional, semantic text carries the meaning, and reduced motion resolves immediately.
5. Service cards were replaced with four numbered editorial rows. The website path leads in orange because it is the primary commercial offer; the remaining rows stay equally reachable without becoming a bento wall.
6. The owner-math result no longer uses a theatrical progress bar. Weekly, four-week, and annual values stay visible in a stable table with tabular numerals, editable currency, exact formula, reset, and an explicit non-forecast limit.
7. The shared contact close now uses a restrained editorial split without a decorative gradient. Call, form, text, email, booking, and the free-first-conversation boundary remain visible.
8. In the built 390×844 page, both primary decisions end at 358.4px, contact begins 2.81 screens down, the full page is 6.70 screens, horizontal overflow is zero, and the browser console is clean.
9. At 320×568, the hero still puts both decisions, Text, Email, Form, and human hours inside the first screen. The desktop-only path and calculator are not mounted, and the page has no horizontal overflow.
10. Service thumbnails now leave the mobile index instead of squeezing each owner answer into a narrow column. At 320px the service section fell from 2,007.5px to 1,379.4px while retaining all four destinations and outcomes.
11. The 320px menu is viewport-bounded and independently scrollable, so Call and Text remain reachable while the body is locked. The sticky help bar stays at 79.7px against its 80px content/focus reservation.
12. A dedicated 320×568 browser contract now protects first-screen help, the mobile-only payload boundary, the shortened service index, the 80px sticky bar, the scrollable menu, and the no-overflow/page-length budgets.

## Result

The built homepage matches the selected reference direction while remaining recognizably Little Fight: direct, slightly snarky, proof-led, owner-safe, and much faster to act on than a typical award-site scroll spectacle.
